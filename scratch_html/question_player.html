A train 150m long passes a pole in 15 seconds and another train of the same length travelling in the opposite direction in 12 seconds. The speed of the second train is:

cancel

Incorrect

Explanation

Speed of the first train = 150 / 15 = 10 m/sec.

Let the speed of the second train be 'x' m/sec.
Relative speed = (10 + x) m/sec.
Distance to be covered = 150 + 150 = 300 m.

Therefore, 300 / (10 + x) = 12
=> 10 + x = 25
=> x = 15 m/sec.
